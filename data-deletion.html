What Data We Hold About You

When you interact with our WhatsApp Business service, we store the following data in our secure database:

Data Type Description Deletable?
📱 Phone Number Your WhatsApp number used to contact us ✅ Fully Deleted
👤 Display Name Your name as shared by WhatsApp/Meta ✅ Fully Deleted
💬 Message History Content of messages sent and received ✅ Fully Deleted
🆔 WhatsApp ID Unique identifier assigned by Meta ✅ Fully Deleted
🕒 Timestamps Date/time of first and last contact ✅ Fully Deleted
📋 Business Records Legal transaction records (if applicable) ⚖️ Kept (Saudi law requires 5 yrs)

⚠️ Note: We can delete data from our own systems, but we cannot delete data held by Meta/WhatsApp. Please refer to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy for Meta's data deletion process.

📋

How to Request Data Deletion

Follow these simple steps to submit your data deletion request:

1

Send an Email

Email us at privacy@greenconcrete.com with the subject line shown below.

2

Include Your Phone Number

Include the WhatsApp phone number (with country code) that you used to contact us, so we can locate your data.

3

We Verify & Process

We will verify your identity, locate your data, and process the deletion within 30 days.

4

Receive Confirmation

We will send a confirmation email once your data has been successfully deleted from our systems.

✉️

Email Template — Copy & Send

You can copy and use this template when emailing us:

To: privacy@greenconcrete.com Subject: Data Deletion Request — [Your Phone Number] Dear Green Concrete Company Privacy Team, I am writing to request the deletion of all personal data you hold about me in connection with your WhatsApp Business service. My WhatsApp Phone Number: +[Your Country Code][Your Number] (e.g. +966 55 866 4650) Please confirm: 1. All personal data (phone number, messages, contact records) has been deleted from your systems. 2. The date of deletion. I understand that legally required business records may be retained as required by Saudi Arabian law. Thank you, [Your Name]

📋 Copy the above, fill in your details, and send to privacy@greenconcrete.com

Deletion Timeline
