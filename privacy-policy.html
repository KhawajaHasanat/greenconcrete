How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • ✅ Sending WhatsApp notifications — order confirmations, updates, and business communications
  • ✅ Responding to your inquiries — replying to messages you send us via WhatsApp
  • ✅ Customer support — managing and tracking your service requests
  • ✅ Record keeping — storing conversation history in our secure database for business operations
  • ✅ Service improvement — analyzing communication patterns to improve response quality

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to any third parties for commercial gain.

4

Third-Party Sharing — Meta / WhatsApp

Our WhatsApp communication system is powered by the Meta WhatsApp Business API. By messaging us on WhatsApp, you acknowledge that:

  • 📡 Your messages are transmitted through Meta's (Facebook's) infrastructure
  • 🌐 Meta processes message delivery data in accordance with their own WhatsApp Privacy Policy
  • 🔐 Message content is end-to-end encrypted during transit by WhatsApp
  • 🏢 We store a copy of message content in our own secure database (SQL Server) for business record purposes

We do not share your personal data with any other third parties, advertisers, data brokers, or external services beyond Meta/WhatsApp for message delivery.

Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • 🔍 Right to Access — Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • ✏️ Right to Rectification — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • 🗑️ Right to Deletion — Request deletion of all your personal data from our systems
  • ⛔ Right to Object — Object to how we process your data
  • 📦 Right to Portability — Request your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@greenconcrete.com with the subject line "Data Request — [Your Phone Number]". We will respond within 30 days.

Data Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously. Our security measures include:

  • 📋 Regular security reviews of our systems and access controls
